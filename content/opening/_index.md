<br>
<p>
We are actively recruiting self-motivated <span2><b>Ph.D. Students and Research Assistants</b></span2> to join the <b>Healthcare AI Lab</b> at PolyU DSAI. We also welcome applications from <span2><b>Research Interns (on-site or remote)</b></span2> whose interests closely align with our research.
</p>
<p>
Our lab develops <span2><b>Generalizable, Agentic, and Multimodal AI systems for healthcare</b></span2>. We aim to bridge fundamental AI research and clinical applications by building intelligent systems that can perceive, reason, and assist in complex medical environments.
</p>
<p>
👉 <b>Our latest work and publications are available on <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=oVG2zEkAAAAJ&hl=en">Google Scholar</a>.</b>
</p>

<br>
<h2><b>🔬 Research Pillars</b></h2>
<ul>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.7em;"><span2><b>Medical Foundation Models</b></span2><br><span style="font-size: 0.92em; color: #606872;">Developing multimodal foundation models that integrate medical imaging, surgical video, electronic health records, and biomedical literature to support comprehensive clinical reasoning.</span></li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.7em;"><span2><b>Agentic AI for Healthcare</b></span2><br><span style="font-size: 0.92em; color: #606872;">Building clinical AI agents and intelligent copilots with capabilities in autonomous planning, multimodal reasoning, and video-language-action learning.</span></li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.7em;"><span2><b>Translational AI</b></span2><br><span style="font-size: 0.92em; color: #606872;">Moving beyond algorithm development to build deployable prototypes and conduct rigorous validation in real-world clinical settings.</span></li>
</ul>

<br>
<h2><b>🚀 Why Join Us?</b></h2>
<ul>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.7em;"><span2><b>Frontline Research</b></span2><br><span style="font-size: 0.92em; color: #606872;">Work on emerging topics such as <b>LLM agents, vision-language models, and world models</b>, with close supervision and hands-on research guidance.</span></li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.7em;"><span2><b>Proven Mentorship</b></span2><br><span style="font-size: 0.92em; color: #606872;">We provide systematic training in independent research and support students in preparing for future academic and industrial careers. Previous students under my mentorship have secured Ph.D. positions at leading universities, including <b>Cambridge, Edinburgh, Nottingham, and UIUC</b>.</span></li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.7em;"><span2><b>Clinical Collaboration</b></span2><br><span style="font-size: 0.92em; color: #606872;">Benefit from long-term collaborations with clinicians and hospitals in the United States, Hong Kong, and mainland China, with opportunities to work on clinically meaningful problems using medical data and expert feedback, subject to applicable approvals.</span></li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.7em;"><span2><b>Solid Support</b></span2><br><span style="font-size: 0.92em; color: #606872;">We provide ample GPU resources, international collaboration opportunities, and strong support and guidance for fellowship and scholarship applications.</span></li>
</ul>

<br>
<h2><b>🎯 Requirements</b></h2>
<p>
The following requirements primarily apply to <span2><b>Ph.D. and Research Assistant applicants</b></span2>:
</p>
<ul>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.45em;">A B.S. or M.S. degree in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Data Science, Electrical Engineering, Biomedical Engineering, Mathematics, Automation, or a related field.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.45em;">A solid mathematical foundation, proficiency in Python and PyTorch, and familiarity with modern deep learning architectures.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.45em;">Research experience in computer vision, natural language processing, or deep learning. Prior experience with LLMs, AI agents, or diffusion models is preferred but not required.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.45em;">A genuine interest in healthcare problems and the persistence to tackle challenging, high-impact research questions.</li>
<li style="margin-bottom: 0.45em;">Strong communication skills and the ability to conduct research independently and collaboratively.</li>
</ul>
<p style="font-size: 0.92em; color: #606872;">
Applications for research internships will be considered based on the candidate’s background, availability, and alignment with ongoing projects.
</p>
